## How to Read Papers Efficiently

### Three-Pass Method

**Pass 1 (5 min):** Title + Abstract + Intro (last paragraph) + Conclusion. Look at figure captions. Decide if worth deep reading.

**Pass 2 (1 hour):** Read full text, skip proofs. Write 1-2 sentence summary at each section start. Note what you don't understand.

**Pass 3 (2-4 hours):** Reproduce core experiments with code. Think about assumptions and limitations. Think about improvements.
